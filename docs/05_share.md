# Phase 5: Share

## Purpose

Put the three answers where the director will actually read them.

## What this phase produces

`reports/recommendation_memo.pdf` is two US-letter pages. `dashboard/index.html` is one page. The three headline charts are the model-rate chart, the age-hazard chart, and the spare chart.

## The work

The memo opens with the buy, the stop, and the age. Page one holds the decision table and the hazard curve. Page two holds the spare counts, the action list, and the limits. The full stop list and the interval chart live on the dashboard, because a two-page brief cannot carry every model and still be a brief.

Each dashboard heading is one question from Phase 1.

| View | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Fleet AFR, the standard model, the age, and the spare total | Can she see the answer before she scrolls? |
| Model table and `afr_by_model.png` | Which models should we standardize on, and which should we stop buying? |
| `hazard_by_age.png` and `kaplan_meier.png` | At what age does failure risk rise sharply? |
| `spares_by_fleet_size.png` and the scale table | How many spares should we keep during replacement lead time? |

`quarterly_afr.png`, `afr_by_manufacturer.png`, and `preparation_funnel.png` support the memo and are not a fourth question.

The spare sentence uses the operational split, 7 and 3, not the cost screen's first pick. The memo says in plain language why the cheapest approved model did not become the 80% buy: 23 failures, and no drives in two large datacenters.

## Check

The first paragraph names the WDC model, the Toshiba second source, 13 models to stop, and 4.0 power-on years. A reader who stops there can repeat the recommendation. The page count of the PDF is 2.
